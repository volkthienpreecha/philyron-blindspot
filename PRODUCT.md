# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Reliability engineers and ML staff investigate sensor patterns with little or no corresponding captain reporting. The demo is also intended for an interview walkthrough. The primary workflow is discover reporting gaps, compare evidence and related reports, and save an investigation decision.

## Product Purpose

The reporting-gap workspace groups unstructured captain reports using embeddings, compares those groups with recurring sensor patterns, and prioritizes candidate gaps in reporting. The central question is: what abnormal behavior are maintenance records failing to capture? Human verification determines which cases can become evidence for Philyron's existing ML system. Sparse reporting alone does not establish a hidden failure.

## Brand Personality

Precise, practical, calm. Use Philyron's public palette and typography as the brand reference. This remains an independent synthetic demonstration, not proprietary Philyron software.

## References

The user preferred the first ink-and-teal concept, then requested three very different dashboard directions with a premium white/glassy Apple-like feel. Preserve its functional density in one option and explore distinct compositions in the others. Use the user's attached official Philyron logo, not invented marks. Untitled UI React is permitted for reusable components. Philyron.com supplies brand color and font evidence.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing landing pages, decorative maritime scenery, excessive ornament, and unexplained ML claims. Keep the reference's density and geometry. Do not invent commercial performance figures.

## Design Principles

- Center reporting-gap discovery rather than a separate predictive model.
- Make missing maintenance evidence visible at a glance.
- Distinguish abnormal behavior, a reported issue, and verified failure.
- Put sensor evidence and captain records close to the decision.
- Verified discoveries can feed the existing ML system; do not make retraining the dashboard's main purpose.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable labels and redundant text for status colors. Keep chart labels legible. Keyboard focus and reduced motion are required when implementing. No bespoke accessibility needs have been supplied.
